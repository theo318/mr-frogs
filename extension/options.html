How Overmind enforces this

Every bid runs through priority-ordered rules. The triggered rule and the reason are surfaced on the auction page so policy decisions are visibly explainable, and every check emits an OpenTelemetry span to console.overmindlab.ai.

  • sensitivity_blocked STOP Segment is in a sensitivity tier you've blocked.
  • bid_below_reserve STOP Bid is below your reserve price.
  • anomalous_bid FLAG Bid is more than 3× the segment floor — suspicious overpayment.
  • medium_sensitivity_unverified FLAG Medium-sensitivity sales always require explicit per-bid approval.
  • default PASS All rules passed — bid is auto-sellable.
